Dear Friend,

Mainu samajh nahi aa reha ki gall kitthon shuru karaan, par main dil ton tainu kujh kehna chaunda haan.

Sab ton pehlan, main tethon dil ton maafi mangda haan. Je main kade tere naal galat tareeke naal gall kiti, koi galat gall kahi, jaan meri kise gall naal tainu bura lagga hove, taan I'm genuinely sorry.

Tu meri ik changi dost hai. Tere naal aam gallan karna, hassna te apniyan gallan share karna mainu changa lagda hai. Teri dosti mere layi maayne rakhdi hai.

Shayad main saadi dosti nu lai ke kujh gallan galat samajh baitha si, jaan apni gall sahi tareeke naal samjha nahi paya. Je meri kise gall naal tainu pareshani hoi hove, taan main apni galti mann da haan.

I'm truly sorry for everything.

Mera kade vi tainu dukh pahunchaan jaan uncomfortable feel karvaun da iraada nahi si. Je meri kise gall naal tainu bura lagga, taan main dil ton maafi mangda haan.

Main teri te teriyan feelings di izzat karda haan. Mainu tere ton kise tarah di koi umeed nahi. Bas eh chaunda haan ki saadi dosti vich pehlan vang hi apnapan te respect bani rahe.

Main apni galti samajhda haan te agge ton apniyan gallan da hor dhyaan rakhanga. Tainu jawab den di koi jaldi nahi hai.

Bas ena hi kehna si — I'm really, genuinely sorry.

With sincere apologies,
Your Friend.
